\section{CONCLUSION}
This paper treats runtime recovery knowledge as an attackable part of PCPS restoration. KG-FedMARL separates protected semantic information from assertions inferred online from measurements, controller status, communication state, and recovery outcomes. Provenance-bounded temporal belief updates limit evidence influence, the recovery gate uses those beliefs for semantic prerequisites before authoritative physical verification, and uncertain transitions are quarantined before they can update the deployed policy.

The analysis gives sufficient bounds for belief distortion, semantic false authorization, post-attack correction time, and deployed-policy drift under the trust assumptions stated in Section~III. The controlled experiments remain within those envelopes. In the 20-seed IEEE 33-bus study with medium mixed poisoning, KG-FedMARL reduces the semantic false-authorization rate by 75.0\% relative to Latest-Evidence and by 89.3\% relative to Static-KG; its mean AURC is 0.92\% below the Oracle-AC reference. The feeder ablations are consistent with different roles for provenance capping, temporal forgetting, the semantic gate, and transition quarantine, although the short horizon does not separate the fixed and trust-dependent policy-promotion radii well. On the 4,876-bus realization of the IEEE 8500-node feeder, the revised 32-block configuration reaches 90\% served load at step 18 and full final service in all 20 runs, with 62.4 ms mean authorization-loop latency and a 0.0063 mean fallback rate. Because block granularity, damaged-state construction, and bounded candidate search were changed together, this experiment establishes liveness for the revised configuration but does not attribute the improvement to any one of those changes.

The guarantees remain conditional on authenticated source identities, correct provenance grouping and belief updates, protected semantic and actuation components, containment of the realized state in the verifier's conservative state set, and an uncompromised authoritative verifier. Corruption of these trusted components, or compromise of all independent provenance groups, is outside the present guarantee. The analysis also does not establish global convergence of the underlying MARL optimizer.
