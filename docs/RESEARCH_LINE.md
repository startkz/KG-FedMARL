# Default research line

在恢复所依据的知识本身可能缺失、过时、相互冲突或被攻击者污染时，如何建立一个有界可信的在线知识状态，并限制错误知识向恢复约束和恢复策略传播。

## Consequences for revision

The methodological core should center on adversarial evidence, provenance-aware temporal belief maintenance, risk-aware recovery constraints, and trust-gated online policy adaptation. Federated learning, heterogeneous-agent adapters, shared encoders, credibility aggregation, GAT, and related components are supporting infrastructure and must not be presented collectively as the primary novelty.
